## 🎁 Givelink

**Why it matters**: Nonprofits need goods as much as cash. On Givelink, donors buy real items from a charity's wishlist, and we deliver them to the charity.
- **Live**: https://givelink.app, with nonprofits in Greece and the US
- Charity onboarding, wishlists, donor checkout, and fulfillment through supplier partners
- 67 pull requests merged into Givelink's production codebase since June 2026 (TypeScript, Next.js, React)

**What I've shipped**

*For nonprofits*
- Wishlist tools: duplicate a wishlist into a new draft, filter products by supplier, sort by most requested and most donated
- Donor details export to CSV, including which wishlist each gift came from
- Download and share delivery photos from the dashboard
- A smoother onboarding: redesigned login, fixed address autofill, clear messages naming every missing field, and onboarding emails

*For donors*
- Share the delivery photo straight from a completed donation, and zoom into delivery photos
- Charity logo on in-kind donation receipts, and share cards designed for each charity instead of one static image
- Greek donor names transliterated for readers in other languages

*Growth and discoverability*
- Made 200+ nonprofit pages and the blog readable by Google and AI assistants: structured data, FAQ markup, sitemap, `llms.txt`
- New SEO landing pages, a filterable blog index, press and podcast mentions on the US homepage, and an easier path to self-sign-up

*Data and operations*
- PostHog tracking across the donor site, the nonprofit dashboard and the backend, plus an analysis API for charity data
- Admin tooling: charity table sorting, filters and status counts, purchase-order sheets in the delivery export, Slack alerts

*Internal tool (public repo)*: [Admin Products Page](https://github.com/panoskokmotos/admin-products-page), the catalog table the ops team uses for pricing, markup and stock limits

## 🧪 Side projects

- **[Verified Delivery Copilot](https://verified-delivery-copilot.vercel.app)** ([code](https://github.com/panoskokmotos/verified-delivery-copilot)): an AI agent that checks nonprofit delivery photos before donors see them. It flags AI-generated images, checks that every donated item is in the photo, and screens wishlist products against US and EU recall databases. Built for the Nebius x NVIDIA Global AI Hackathon on Nemotron. TypeScript.
- **[AI for Social Impact tools](https://tools.panoskokmotos.com)** ([code](https://github.com/panoskokmotos/social-impact-tools)): free AI tools for people working on social problems, running on Claude through a Cloudflare Worker. Includes [Impact Compass](https://tools.panoskokmotos.com/compass/), an installable PWA covering 25 world problems with sourced figures and evidence ratings, in English and Greek.
- **[Task Management OS](https://github.com/panoskokmotos/task-management)**: my offline-first personal operating system. Vanilla JS PWA with Supabase sync, a ⌘K command palette, AI daily picks, OKRs and a North Star pace tracker.
- **[Polymath](https://hobbies-match.app)**: matches people by 50+ hobbies. React 18, Tailwind, Butterbase. Live since May 2026.
- **[The Crucible](https://beginning-of-infinity-game.vercel.app)** ([code](https://github.com/panoskokmotos/Beginning-of-Infinity-game)): a game where you defend an idea against an AI interlocutor, built on Deutschian epistemology. Next.js 14 with SSE streaming, MVP complete.
